# tiimo-alternatives

Tiimo is genuinely good at visual daily schedules. People still leave it,
and it is usually for one of three reasons.

## Who should stay with Tiimo
If your day runs on routines and you live on your phone, nothing here beats it. Switching
would cost you the thing you actually use it for.

## The alternatives worth looking at
Each of these solves a different half of the problem, and none solves all of it.

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
