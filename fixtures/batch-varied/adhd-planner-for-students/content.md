# adhd-planner-for-students

If you are studying with ADHD, the planner problem is not that you
lack one. It is that the planner assumes a version of you who updates it.

## The problem for students
Semester deadlines are far away until they are not, and nothing in between reminds you.

## What the workflow looks like
Capture in one place, review once a week, and let the dates do the sorting.

## What changes
You stop rebuilding the system every fortnight, which is where the effort used to go.
