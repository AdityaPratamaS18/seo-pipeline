# what-is-task-paralysis

Task paralysis is the state of knowing exactly what to do and being
unable to begin. It is a starting problem, not a motivation problem.

## How it shows up
It looks like scrolling. It looks like reorganising a list instead of working from it.

## Why it happens
The initiation step is a separate function from the wanting step, and it fails on its own.

## What helps
Reduce the number of decisions between you and the first action.
