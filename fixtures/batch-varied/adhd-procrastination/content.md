# adhd-procrastination

You know the task and you know the deadline, and you still cannot start.
That gap has a name, and it is not laziness.

## Why this is harder than it sounds
Executive function is the set of processes that get you from knowing to doing. In ADHD
brains it is unreliable rather than absent, which is why willpower advice lands so badly.

## What to do in the next ten minutes
Write the task down before you carry it around for an hour. Then shrink the first move
until it is boring. Boring is the point, because boring does not trigger avoidance.

## When this is not the real problem
Sometimes the answer is burnout, or a clinician, and no app fixes either of those.
